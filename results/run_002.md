Router still stuck near uniform distribution (log(4) = 1.386). Confirmed: the stall is a **data problem**, not an LR problem. T_comb at 200 samples simply doesn't carry enough domain-discriminative signal in the pre-LLM embedding space for the router to learn from, regardless of LR.

## Key Findings

- **Router stall is a data-scale problem.** Even 5× higher LR doesn't help the router learn from T_comb at 200 samples. More data is the only real fix.
- **LR 1e-4 is better for Phase 3 at this scale.** The paper's 2e-5 is calibrated for a model with strong priors from 500K+ pretraining samples. At 200 samples, the experts need a larger LR to break out of their identical initialisation.
- **Phase 3 final lm 0.879** is within a reasonable range for a 135M-param model on 200 training samples over 9 epochs.
